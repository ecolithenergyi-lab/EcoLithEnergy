Battery Chemistry

LFP & NMC Technology

LFP

Lithium Iron Phosphate

LFP battery technology is widely valued for safety, thermal stability, durability and long cycle life.

  • Long Cycle Life
  • High Safety
  • Thermal Stability
  • Reliable Performance
NMC

Nickel Manganese Cobalt

NMC battery technology offers high energy density and is suitable for applications where compact and lightweight energy storage is important.

  • High Energy Density
  • Compact Design
  • Lightweight Solution
  • Strong Performance
